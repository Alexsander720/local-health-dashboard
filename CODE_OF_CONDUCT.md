# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

Be respectful, specific, and constructive. This project handles sensitive
health-related software, so discussions must protect privacy and avoid
presenting uncertain observations as medical facts.

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing, publishing another
person's health data, and knowingly unsafe medical claims are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, issues, or
other participation that violates this standard. Serious or repeated
violations may result in a temporary or permanent ban.

For conduct concerns, open a minimal issue requesting private contact. Do not
include private health data or other sensitive evidence in a public report.
